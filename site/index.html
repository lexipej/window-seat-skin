Ground track

The dot marks the point directly below the station. Each line is a pass, blue where the ground was in daylight, violet at night. The list of passes does the same with the keyboard.

No orbit data, chapters or captions: this pass plays on the plain skin, as an ordinary video would. The sky stays at night.

Six passes

One orbit, cut into six windows

Each pass is a stretch of a real orbit from 2026. The strip under each name is its daylight, worked out from the station’s position and the sun’s.

    Design notes

    Six stops along the ground track

    The player is a seat by the window. Its controls report where the station is and whether the ground below is in sunlight, and they stay legible over bright cloud and dark ocean.

    1. 01 The seek bar is the pass

      With orbit data, the bar shows the light on the ground below the station: blue for day, violet for night, blended through twilight. A tick marks each sunrise and sunset. Without orbit data it is a plain track, split by chapters.

    2. 02 The accent follows the sun

      The accent is gold while the sun is more than 6° up, coral within 6° of the horizon and green below. It is computed from orbital data at the station’s position, never sampled from the picture. This page’s sky follows the same number.

    3. 03 Icons in two solid tones

      Each icon is a solid shape over a second layer at 45% opacity, drawn on a 20 px grid. Filled shapes hold up over bright cloud better than thin outlines. Parts move with state: speaker waves retract, caption marks draw in.

    4. 04 Overflight lists the places

      Chapters name the places the station passes over. Overflight (P) lists them as a route: passed places filled, the current one ringed, the rest hollow. Choosing a place seeks to it, and a callout names the place below.

    5. 05 A porthole on the globe

      In fullscreen, G opens this globe inside the player as a round porthole in three sizes, and it drops to a corner when the controls hide. The dot is the point directly below the station; the camera may be looking elsewhere.

    6. 06 Readable over any footage

      Controls sit on dark tinted glass: text and icons meet 4.5:1, tracks and focus rings 3:1. Focus is a 2 px ring in the accent and touch targets grow to 44 px. Reduced motion, more contrast and forced colours are all honoured.

    Reuse

    Take the seat

    Window Seat is a Video.js v10 HTML skin made for the Video.js Skins Design Challenge. Copy the skin folder into a project, then give each player its markup and a media element. Chapters, captions and orbit data are optional.
